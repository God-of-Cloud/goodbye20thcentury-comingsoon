The Hodeye project dialog follows the November 9 editorial handoff and ordered shot list. It retains the portfolio’s white background, fine rules, numbered captions, circular navigation and next-project footer. The homepage introduces the approved primary wordmark and Flower Market artwork.

The supplied rotating devices remain secondary to these captures.

## Sources and presentation copies

Approved branding, illustration, native icon and apparel exports were copied without retouching. Native screenshots were captured on November 9, 2026 from the installed local Hodeye build on the iPhone 18 Pro Max / iOS 27.0 Simulator. The two Home states, Chat and Profile captures are unchanged PNGs; the action-placement detail is a CSS viewport into the first Home capture.

Website captures come from the existing local Hodeye website’s built output, served read-only. Desktop captures used 1440px-wide viewports at 2× resolution. Mobile captures used 390×844 at 2×. The journal shows the later photographic treatment, not the earlier “Editorial Black Love Journal” concept. Its desktop presentation and footer detail are crops of the same light-appearance capture; the mobile presentation is the complete native responsive website capture. Dating captures are unchanged desktop/mobile screenshots. No website content was composited from separate designs.

Two technical presentation derivatives accompany the unchanged exports:

- The approved 264-frame APNG wordmark animation was transcoded to H.264 at its original 20 fps / 13.2-second timing, over the approved evergreen field, for browser playback.
- The filled Home SVG isolates the exact Regular-M path from the supplied SF Symbol template, with a presentation viewBox. Its geometry was not redrawn.

The source app, source website, approved exports and Figma masters were not edited. Raw captures and local provenance records remain in the ignored `source-assets/hodeye-captures/` and `design-review/hodeye/` folders. `hodeye-editorial-assets.json` records the deployed asset sizes and SHA-256 hashes.

## Claims and disclosures

The title identifies an independent, pre-release native iOS app in progress. Native figures identify local demonstration content, with messaging and service actions unconnected to a live service. Subscription, discovery placement and communication mechanics are presented as proposals, not shipped capabilities. The illustration chapter identifies AI-assisted brand illustration; the final image identifies an apparel mockup. Brand motion is distinguished from the current static native launch treatment. The journal’s editorial serif masthead remains distinct from the custom product wordmark.
